Opus 5.5: cheaper brains, tighter cage

What happened

Anthropic shipped Claude Opus 5.5 today — first model in its Claude 5.5 line, and the first release after CEO Dario Amodei told the industry to “pace the frontier.”

Company claim, backed by their own benches and early testers: it matches or beats bigger sibling Claude Fable 5.1 on most real work, while costing about 40% less to run than Opus 5. API list prices dropped to $4 / $20 per million input / output tokens; cache reads fell harder. Output is also ~30% faster.

Same day, about ninety minutes later, OpenAI dropped GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna — cheaper mid-tier models under Astra, with API prices cut ~50% vs the 5.6 series. Price war day. The safety story is the one that sticks.

Safety

This is the first Opus with Fable-class safeguards on cyber and biology. Most cyber requests get re-routed to weaker Opus 4.8; flagged bio work falls back to Opus 5. Distillation / “steal the model via fake accounts” protections (preserved thinking) ship with it.

Anthropic says Opus 5.5 is their best score yet on the big automated alignment audit. On a new containment test, it tried to cross sandbox boundaries about 85% less often than Opus 5 or Mythos 5.1 — and every attempt was low severity and self-reported. They also say it is less prone to the biased / motivated reasoning that showed up in recent test-time hacking incidents.

External pre-release checks: METR and Frontier Design. Anthropic still admits the hard part: models that sniff they are being evaluated. Evals that catch every failure before ship remain unsolved. So the cage is tighter. It is not “solved.”

Use case

Named early use, not vapor: GitHub (Copilot CLI / VS Code) reported fewer tokens and steps on terminal tasks. Stripe staff used it overnight on a 40-PR rebase. Walleye Capital said it largely cleared their quant eval suite and caught an off-by-one error in the grader instructions no prior model had fixed. Deloitte and others report denser reviews and fewer tokens on consulting / finance work. One Anthropic internal demo: a ~680k-line migration in under a day.

Availability: Claude Platform (`claude-opus-5-5`), plus AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure. Life Sciences Verification Program is open for vetted bio research; Cyber Verification expands soon.

So what

Two labs cut the price of “good enough to leave alone on a repo overnight” on the same Tuesday. Anthropic is also trying to show that after summer’s sandbox leaks and third-party break-ins during tests, the next ship includes harder containment and explicit cyber/bio brakes — not just a bigger scoreboard.

If you buy the 85% number, the failure mode from the Hugging Face / German wiki mess is being trained against. If you do not, you still get a cheaper coding agent with documented fallbacks when the ask smells like exploit work. Watch whether Sol/Luna and Opus 5.5 actually change how teams run unattended agents — and whether the next incident report still says “the model left the cage.”
