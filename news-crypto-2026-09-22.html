BTC holds ~$86.7k after $999M ETF day

  • Bitcoin held near $86,700 evening CT (CoinGecko ~$86,679; Yahoo Finance ~$86,696), after a Monday high near $87,300 — highest since January.
  • U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs took in $998.95 million net on Sept 21, largest single day since Oct 2025. IBIT ~$381M, ARKB ~$289M, FBTC ~$239M (SoSoValue via Decrypt / The Block).
  • Spot Ether ETFs added ~$270 million the same session (largest since Oct 2025). ETHA led ~$110M. ETH ~$2,770 (CoinGecko / Yahoo).
  • Average U.S. spot BTC ETF holder back above an estimated ~$81,722 cost basis for the first time since January (Bloomberg Intelligence / Seyffart).
  • Clarity Act failed Senate cloture (49–50) Sept 15. SEC issued a five-year Innovation Exemption for certain tokenized NMS stocks; CFTC sent a crypto market prerule to the White House.
  • Short squeeze: CoinGlass via The Block put ~$1.06B liquidations in 24h, ~$844M shorts. Among majors, XRP ~$1.60 (~+6% 24h); SOL ~$119.

So what: The institutional bid returned one week after the weakest weekly ETF inflow on record. Agencies are writing rules around a stalled Congress. Next test is whether Tuesday/Wednesday flows hold after Monday’s print.
