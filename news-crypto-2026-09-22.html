BTC back above $86k on ETF flood

  • Bitcoin printed a high near $87,350 on Sept 21, then settled in the mid-$85k–$86k range. Spot price ~$86,000–$86,500.
  • U.S. spot Bitcoin ETFs took in ~$999 million net on Monday — largest single day since Oct 2025. BlackRock IBIT led (~$381M), followed by ARKB (~$289M) and FBTC (~$239M).
  • Ethereum ETFs also flipped hard positive: ~$270 million net inflows the same day. ETH traded ~$2,700–$2,760.
  • Clarity Act failed Senate cloture (49-50 range) last week. Agencies moved without it: SEC issued a temporary Innovation Exemption for tokenized NMS stocks via permissioned AMMs; CFTC sent a crypto market prerule to the White House.
  • ~$1 billion in liquidations over 24 hours, majority shorts. Crypto market cap reclaimed ~$3T.
  • Broader risk-on: softer oil, lower yields, Wall Street rally spilled into crypto.

So what: Institutional bid is back and agencies are writing rules around Congress. Price recovered the post-Clarity/Fed-hike damage in days. Watch whether the ETF flow holds a second day.
